# sales-dashboard
# 📊 Sales Dashboard | Power BI Analytics Project

1) Project Title / Headline

**Sales Performance Dashboard – Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard using Power BI**

---

2) Short Description / Purpose

The **Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Power BI** to analyze and monitor business sales performance.

This dashboard enables users to track **sales trends, profit analysis, category performance, and regional insights** through interactive visualizations, helping organizations make informed business decisions.

---

3) Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following technologies:

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data Visualization & Dashboard Development
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated Measures & KPIs
- **Power Query** – Data Cleaning & Transformation
- **Excel / CSV Dataset** – Data Source Integration
- **Data Modeling** – Relationship Management & Data Structure

---

4) Data Source

**Dataset:** Business Sales Dataset

The dataset contains information related to:

- Sales Transactions
- Revenue & Profit
- Product Categories
- Customer Segments
- Orders & Quantity
- Regional Sales Data

The data was used for sales performance tracking and business analysis.

---

5) Features / Highlights

🔹 Business Problem

Businesses require an efficient way to monitor sales performance, analyze profitability, and identify high-performing products, categories, and regions.

---
 🔹 Goal of the Dashboard

The objective of this dashboard is to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Analyze profit and revenue trends
- Compare product category performance
- Track regional business growth
- Support data-driven business decisions

---

🔹 Key Dashboard Features

✅ **KPI Cards**  
Displays important business metrics such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Orders, and Revenue.

✅ **Sales Trend Analysis**  
Visualizes sales performance over time to identify growth patterns and trends.

✅ **Category Performance Analysis**  
Analyzes sales and profit contribution across different product categories.

✅ **Regional Insights**  
Compares sales performance across different geographical regions.

✅ **Interactive Filters & Slicers**  
Allows users to dynamically explore and filter dashboard data.

✅ **Advanced Visualizations**  
Includes:

- Bar Charts
- Column Charts
- Donut Charts
- Treemaps
- KPI Cards
- Interactive Visual Reports

---

### 🔹 Business Impact & Insights

- Improved visibility into business performance
- Faster analysis of sales trends
- Better understanding of profitable categories
- Enhanced strategic planning and decision-making
